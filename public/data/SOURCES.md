# Map data sources

- `land.json`: Türkiye land polygons from geoBoundaries gbOpen **TUR ADM0**, https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries (CC BY 4.0, derived from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). Simplified here with Douglas-Peucker at 0.0002° (≈20 m) and rounded to five decimals: 440 polygons and ~66,000 points, including the islands. Islands smaller than 2,000 m² are dropped. Parcels are cut against this coastline, so a parcel never extends into the sea; the remaining error is the ~20 m simplification plus OpenStreetMap's own coastline accuracy. It replaced the earlier Natural Earth `turkey.json`, which carried only 3,640 points for the whole country (about 2 km per coastal segment) and left parcels floating offshore. It is an administrative boundary: inland water (lakes, reservoirs and the Bosphorus) is **not** carved out of it, so the lake layer below does that job.
- `places.json`: 81 provinces and 973 district coordinate records from https://github.com/BuNickTamYirmiHarfli/turkey-cities-districts-json/blob/main/cities.json . The author explicitly offers these factual location records for map/API projects. Original field names retained. Coordinates are community-maintained, not independently surveyed or guaranteed current.
- `landuse.json` bundles three real-world layers used for zoning, clipped to Türkiye:
  - **Yerleşik alanlar (`urban`)**: Natural Earth 10m urban areas, https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/ (public domain), retrieved through https://github.com/martynafford/natural-earth-geojson . These are satellite-derived built-up footprints, simplified here to ~25 m. They cover cities and towns but miss some smaller or linear settlements (for example Rize), so district records fill the gaps.
  - **Göller (`water`)**: Natural Earth 10m lakes plus 10m European lakes (Van, Tuz, Beyşehir, Eğirdir, İznik, Sapanca, Ulubat, Manyas, Bafa, Çıldır, Burdur, Hazar, Keban, Atatürk and Seyhan reservoirs among 56 bodies). No parcel is placed on one. These outlines are ~1 km-scale, far coarser than the coastline, so a parcel next to a lake can still sit slightly inside its real shore; small lakes, rivers and seasonal water are not covered at all.
  - **İlçe kayıtları (`towns`)**: 973 district population and area figures from https://github.com/ubeydeozdmr/turkiye-api (TÜİK ADNKS derived), joined by name to the district coordinates already bundled in `places.json`. Three district names misspelled in `places.json` were matched by nearest-name fallback.

  How they are used: a parcel inside a built-up footprint, or within a settlement radius estimated from its district population (assuming ~4,000 residents per built-up km²), is treated as **arsa** (imarlı). Floor limits and prices scale with the district's real population and density; everything further out stays **tarla/arazi**. This is a documented approximation, not a municipal development plan, and it carries no legal meaning.
- Streets and place/neighborhood labels: OpenFreeMap Liberty, https://openfreemap.org/quick_start/ . Attribution: OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles, © OpenStreetMap contributors; OSM data under ODbL. Remote tiles are not bundled in this repository.
- Satellite imagery: Esri World Imagery, https://services.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer . Metadata attribution retrieved for this release: Source: Esri, Vantor, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User Community. Provider imagery is displayed remotely and not copied into the repository. Resolution, dates and coverage vary. Confirm the provider's current service terms and commercial capacity before a paid launch.

No public geocoder is called. Search runs against the bundled province/district index. Neighborhood labels depend on the basemap; searching every neighborhood by name is not implemented.

Parcel boundaries and shapes are generated by the game, not surveyed: they are not cadastral boundaries. Land class, zoning permissions, floor limits and prices are derived from the real settlement data listed above, but the derivation is the game's own model — it is not a municipal development plan (imar planı), a title record or price data, and nothing here indicates real ownership, planning permission or investment value.
